Description on Jollof-Rice

Jollof Rice is one of West Africa's most celebrated dishes, known for its vibrant red color, rich tomato flavor, and fragrant blend of spices.

The rice is cooked in a seasoned tomato and pepper sauce, allowing it to absorb all the flavors while remaining fluffy. It is often served with grilled chicken, fried plantains, beef, fish, or salad, making it a staple at family meals and celebrations.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain parboiled rice
  • 5 large tomatoes
  • 1-2 onion
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 2 seasoning cubes
  • Some Salt

Steps

  1. Blend tomatoes, bell peppers, scotch bonnet peppers, and half the onion until smooth.
  2. Heat vegetable oil in a large pot.
  3. Fry the remaining chopped onion until soft.
  4. Add tomato paste and fry for 3–5 minutes.
  5. Pour in the blended tomato mixture.
  6. Cook for 15–20 minutes until the sauce thickens and the oil begins to separate.
  7. Stir in garlic, ginger, thyme, curry powder, paprika, seasoning cubes, salt, and pepper.
  8. Add the chicken stock and bay leaves.
  9. Bring the mixture to a gentle boil.
  10. Wash the rice thoroughly and add it to the pot.
  11. Stir well to coat every grain.
  12. Cover the pot tightly with foil and then the lid to trap steam.
  13. Cook over low heat for 30–40 minutes.
  14. Stir occasionally and add a little hot water if necessary.
  15. Continue cooking until the rice is tender.
  16. Fluff with a fork before serving.
