Description on Egusi Soup

Egusi Soup is a traditional West African soup made primarily from ground melon seeds (egusi), which give the soup its rich, nutty flavor and thick texture.

It is typically cooked with leafy vegetables, palm oil, peppers, onions, assorted meats, fish, and flavorful seasonings. Egusi soup is commonly enjoyed with pounded yam, fufu, eba (garri), semolina, or amala, making it a hearty and satisfying meal.

Ingredients

  • cups ground egusi (melon seeds)
  • ½ cup palm oil
  • 500 g assorted meat (beef, tripe, cow skin, etc.)
  • 200 g smoked fish
  • 1 cup dried stockfish (optional)
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2–3 scotch bonnet peppers
  • 2 red bell peppers
  • 3 cups chopped spinach, ugu (fluted pumpkin), or bitter leaf
  • 3 seasoning cubes
  • 2 tsp ground crayfish
  • Salt to taste
  • 4 cups meat stock
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp ground ginger

Steps

  1. Cook the assorted meat with onions, seasoning cubes, salt, and water until tender.
  2. Soak and clean the stockfish if using.
  3. Wash and debone the smoked fish.
  4. Blend the peppers and garlic into a smooth mixture.
  5. Heat the palm oil in a large pot over medium heat.
  6. Add the blended pepper mixture and cook for about 10 minutes.
  7. Stir in the ground crayfish.
  8. Gradually add the ground egusi while stirring to prevent lumps.
  9. Cook for 8–10 minutes until the egusi begins to set.
  10. Pour in the meat stock.
  11. Add the cooked meat, smoked fish, and stockfish.
  12. Simmer for 15–20 minutes so the flavors combine.
  13. Stir in the chopped leafy vegetables.
  14. Cook for another 3–5 minutes until the vegetables are just tender.
  15. Taste and adjust the seasoning if needed./li>
  16. Remove from the heat and serve hot with pounded yam, eba, fufu, semolina, or amala.
